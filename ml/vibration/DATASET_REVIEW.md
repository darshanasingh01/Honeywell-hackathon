# Vibration and knock dataset review

Reviewed 2026-10-16; Mendeley endpoint inspection attempted 2026-10-16. No
candidate below is downloaded or cleared for supervised training. Article
open-access licensing does not automatically license the underlying raw
measurements.

| Candidate | Signal and labels | Size / rate | IDs, license, leakage, decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Diesel-CNG dual-fuel engine knock signal (Mendeley Data, 2021)](https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/jzmhw4mbn7/1) | Actual piezoelectric knock-sensor signal mounted near cylinder 1 on a diesel-CNG dual-fuel internal-combustion engine. Published metadata describes varying diesel/CNG ratios, not explicit normal/knock/fault class labels. | Raw knock signal; file count and archive size are not exposed in static public metadata. Landing-page file list and Download All control remained in loading/disabled state. Recorded at 1-degree crank-angle intervals at a constant 1400 rpm. This is crank-angle sampling, not a published uniform Hz rate; nominal conversion at exactly 1400 rpm would be 8400 samples/s, but do not substitute that for verified timestamps. Units/scaling are not stated. | Dataset license is CC0 1.0. Engine/session/measurement IDs and cycle grouping are not described in the metadata. Likely one engine test context; fuel-ratio/run leakage is a major risk if adjacent cycles are split. Best candidate for knock-signal novelty work after checking file metadata and grouping; not currently a verified supervised fault dataset. **Not downloaded:** Mendeley API metadata calls returned HTTP 401 without OAuth; ZIP metadata and ZIP redirect HEAD checks returned HTTP 404. Total size could not be verified, so the 1 GB pre-download requirement could not be satisfied. |
| [Smartphone engine misfire, Lima Filho et al. (2022)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9315533/) | Actual smartphone accelerometer vibration on a Ford Fiesta 2005; healthy, one-piston misfire, two-piston misfire. | Three conditions are documented, signals acquired at 200 samples/s for about one minute each. Exact number of raw recordings is not stated in the article. | Single vehicle and neutral/idling setup; no reusable session/vehicle partition IDs described. Raw data available from authors upon request, not direct public download. Article is CC BY, but raw-data reuse permission is not stated. High session leakage risk if windows from same minute are split. Lead for an author request, not currently trainable. |
| [Spark ignition engine misfire, Venkatesh et al. (2022)](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1155/2022/7606896) | Actual engine-head accelerometer signal on a 4-cylinder gasoline IC engine; normal and c1/c2/c3/c4 single-cylinder misfire. Misfire was induced by cutting spark to a cylinder. | 24 kHz, 8192 samples per described acquisition, 100 plot images per class (500 images total) generated from acquired vibration signals. Raw recording count cannot be determined from the article. | One engine and 1500 rpm/no-load; source-session/vehicle IDs and group split are not reported. Data availability says data are included within the article and additional information on request; raw time series are not directly downloadable as a dataset. Article copyright/license is distinct from data rights. Very high image/window leakage risk; do not use reported image accuracy as a reproducible raw-signal benchmark. |
| [Automotive misfire with smartphone through wavelet/chaos (2022)](https://www.mdpi.com/1424-8220/22/14/5166) | Same study as first row, actual engine vibration, healthy/1FP/2FP. | 200 Hz, ~1-minute scenarios; exact recording counts not published. | Raw data on request; CC BY applies to article. The source describes one 2005 Ford Fiesta and no session IDs. See first row; listed separately only as the primary publication page, not as an independent dataset. |
| [CILAMCE 2021 engine ignition-failure study](https://publicacoes.softaliza.com.br/cilamce2021/article/download/5801/4865/2022) | Real Ford Zetec-Roman 2006 engine, MPU6050 accelerometer, induced ignition failures by disconnecting spark plugs. It describes healthy signal and cylinder-specific ignition failure; the paper indicates 30 signals of 30 seconds for each condition, but exact named class inventory and total signal count are not clearly tabulated. | 512 Hz; acceleration units/calibration are not specified. | One car/engine at about 900 rpm; recordings made on different days by condition. No files, IDs, or reuse license found with the paper. Published metrics have unclear split provenance and may be signal/window-level. Not directly downloadable or ready for training. |
| [Venkatesh et al. raw-data note](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9287110/) | Same 5 categories as row two; actual accelerometer signal. | 24 kHz, 100 derived plot images/class; raw file count unspecified. | Data available in article and further information on request. No public raw dataset archive or data license verified. |

Industrial bearing and rotating-machine benchmarks were excluded because they
are not automotive engine knock/vibration data. Public research descriptions
show that labelled engine signals exist, but the raw data located here are
author-request or article-only. No dataset is presently suitable for a
reproducible, vehicle/session-grouped supervised baseline without obtaining
the raw data and confirming reuse rights and IDs.

## References supporting the review

- Smartphone study: 200 Hz acquisitions, Ford Fiesta, induced misfire classes, and author-request raw data are stated in the [article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9315533/).
- Mendeley record: piezoelectric knock sensor, fuel-ratio variation, 1400 rpm, 1-degree crank-angle interval, and CC0 1.0 are stated on its [dataset page](https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/jzmhw4mbn7/1); class labels, files/counts, and units are not published there.
- Spark ignition study: sensor sensitivity, 24 kHz, 1500 rpm, five conditions, and 500 derived images are described in the [article](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1155/2022/7606896).
- CILAMCE study: engine, MPU6050, 512 Hz, 900 rpm, and induced ignition failures are described in the [proceedings paper](https://publicacoes.softaliza.com.br/cilamce2021/article/download/5801/4865/2022).
