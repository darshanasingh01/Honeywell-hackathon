Published VAFD counts and taxonomy are described in the [2025 Machines paper](https://www.mdpi.com/2075-1702/13/4/258) and the later [dataset refinement paper](https://www.mdpi.com/2075-1702/13/10/888). These are published inventory counts, not a fresh local WAV integrity or duplicate audit. Both projects describe curated sound collections with varied sources; VAFD should not be represented as a uniformly controlled engine-bench dataset. The second paper states that its wider collection uses public datasets and YouTube-derived material.

## Recommendation

Do not merge any candidate with HL-CEAD. VAFD is the strongest technical lead because it exposes fault and general-vehicle WAVs with published class counts and label-bearing filenames. It is **not legally cleared** on the evidence available here. Seek written reuse terms plus a source/session mapping and duplicate audit before download or model training. Until that is resolved, keep novelty outputs described only as acoustic-reference deviations, not detected faults.
